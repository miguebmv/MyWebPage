Robotics Engineer & Big Data Scientist

I was born in Granada, Spain, in 2000, and spent my childhood there, attending school in my hometown. Later, I pursued my studies in Malaga, specializing in Robotics Engineering. After a year of exchange at Miami University in Oxford, Ohio, I decided to pursue my passion for data by completing my master's in Big Data Analytics in San Diego, California.
